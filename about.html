Our story

We started as what we are: architects.

We graduated as architects and from day one we knew we wanted to do something different. Not build buildings — build the image that makes them desirable.

Before founding Imabox we spent almost a year traveling the world. China, Korea, Japan, Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, Colombia. Not as tourists — as architects with wide-open eyes. That gave our perspective something no studio teaches: real-world context.

In 2018 we founded Imabox in Montevideo. Not as a visualization studio, but as a creative agency that takes full ownership of a project's entire image — from brand identity to launch day.

North-facing orientation doesn't sell an apartment. Morning sunlight streaming through the window as you have your coffee, does. With every image, every video, every cinemagraph, we build the experience of living something that doesn't yet exist — so whoever sees it desires it before the first brick is laid.
