About me

Architect, 3D artist —
and the code in between.

Montevideo, Uruguay

I'm Rodrigo Figueredo, architect and senior 3D artist. I studied architecture at Universidad de la República, and today I split my time between Gómez Platero — creating concept imagery in the 3D team for some of the region's largest projects — and Imabox, the creative agency I co-founded in 2018. Everything on the Works page came out of that practice.

China · Korea · Japan · Europe

In 2019 I spent almost a year traveling — China, Korea, Japan, Italy, Switzerland, Germany, France, Colombia. Not as a tourist: as an architect with wide-open eyes. Walking through buildings I had only known from books changed how I understand scale, light and cities — and that perspective is behind every image I make.

Craft + code

I've always lived between two worlds: the artistic and the technical. Today that means pairing visualization craft with code and AI — writing my own tools in JavaScript and Python, building 3D experiences for the web, and using generative models as one more brush. The video beside this text? I made it with AI.
