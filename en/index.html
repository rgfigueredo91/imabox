Frequently asked questions

Everything you need
to know before starting.

Do I need finished plans to get started?
No. We work from sketches and early designs. The sooner we join, the more value we add to the design and communication process. We just need a reference of what you want to do.
Do you only work in Uruguay?
No. We work remotely with clients in over 7 countries. The process is 100% digital — from brief to final file delivery.
How long does a project take?
We adapt to your deadlines. If it's urgent, a single image can be ready from one day to the next. When there's more time, we use it to add review rounds and polish every detail. Your project sets the pace — everything is defined in the initial proposal with clear dates.
Do you have packages or is it custom work?
Both options. We have individual services (renders only, branding only) and complete integral campaigns. The most common is that we put together a custom proposal based on the type and size of the project.
How much does it cost? How do you quote?
Every project is quoted individually based on the number of images, complexity and scope. Request a quote with no commitment and within 24–48 hours we send you a detailed proposal. We work with fixed budgets: you know the total cost before starting, no surprises.
Can I use the images in advertising? Who owns the rights?
The images are yours. You can use them without limits in advertising, listing portals, social media and sales material. We only reserve the right to show them in our portfolio — and if the project is confidential, not even that: we agree on it by contract.
Do you handle confidential projects?
Yes, it's common. Many projects reach us before their public launch. If you need it we sign an NDA and the project doesn't appear in our portfolio until you announce it.
Who maintains the 3D Platform and what happens as units are sold?
The platform stays online throughout the sales period. We update unit availability, prices and material whenever you need — updates are included and you only pay for the hosting required to keep it online. It works in any browser, mobile included, with nothing to install.
